Privacy

Farm Records is a private record-keeping tool used by one farm and its staff. It is not offered to the public.

What it stores

Records about the farm's animals (arrivals, births, treatments, weights, deaths, sales), optional photos of the animals, and the first name of the staff member who made each entry.

Where it is stored

Entries are kept on the device that recorded them until they upload. They are then stored in a Google Sheet, with photos in Google Drive, both owned by the farm's Google account. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google permissions

The connected Google script can only access the one Google Sheet it is attached to and the photo files it creates itself in Google Drive. It cannot see any other files, email or account information.

Removing data

The farm owner can edit or delete any record directly in the Google Sheet, and can remove the script's access at any time from their Google Account under Security → Third-party apps.

Contact

Questions about these records should go to the farm owner.
